# Entry requirements for Colombian passport holders, Panama, Costa Rica and Nicaragua (overland by bicycle), plus the Costa Rica to El Salvador ferry and CA-4. Status as of October 2026

Research date: 2026-10-17. Method note: the web-page fetch tool failed for every domain (DNS errors), so these findings come from search-engine result summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages. Exact wording, especially on official PDFs (migracion.go.cr, embajadadecostarica.org, mint.gob.ni), should be checked against the originals before the final guide is published. Source dates are given in brackets. "Undated" means the search result showed no date.

## Panama: visa, length of stay, proof of funds, onward ticket, leaving by land at Paso Canoas, exit fees

### Takeaway
Colombians do not need a visa for Panama as tourists. They get up to 90 days. They must show at least USD 500 in funds and proof that they will leave the country. A bus ticket only to the border does not count as onward travel. Cyclists say these checks are applied unevenly at Paso Canoas. I found no source for a Panamanian exit fee at the land border.

### Cited Findings
- Colombian tourists do not need a visa for Panama as long as they stay no more than 90 days [2026 guide] — [Holafly](https://esim.holafly.com/es/blog/planificar-viaje/requisitos-viajar-colombia-panama/).
- Panama cut the tourist stay for Colombians from 180 days to 90 days. The article is old (undated in the results, several years old), so 90 days is the current reference and 180 days is outdated — [El Tiempo](https://www.eltiempo.com/mundo/latinoamerica/panama-le-reduce-estadia-a-turistas-de-colombia-92734); see also [NDM, "duración máxima de tu estadía en Panamá"](https://www.ndm.com.pa/es/estadia-en-panama-como-turista/) [undated].
- Funds: travellers must show at least USD 500 (or equivalent) at entry, by cash, credit card, bank reference, employment letter or traveller's cheques — [Embassy of Panama (Washington), Visas](https://www.embassyofpanama.org/visas) [undated]; also listed in [IATI 2026 guide](https://www.iatiglobal.com/co/blog/requisitos-viajar-panama-desde-colombia/).
- Sources disagree on passport validity. The Panamanian Embassy says a minimum of 3 months, while Tocumen Airport asks for at least 6 months. Guides therefore recommend 6 months [2026 guides] — [Holafly](https://esim.holafly.com/es/blog/planificar-viaje/requisitos-viajar-colombia-panama/); [Caribe Travel Tours 2026](https://www.caribetraveltours.com/requisitos-de-viaje/panama-desde-colombia).
- Onward ticket: 2026 guides say travellers must show an air, bus or boat ticket proving they will leave within the permitted stay. Migration may refuse entry if this cannot be shown. (This comes from search summaries of the 2026 guides above; I could not open the pages to see which guide says it.) — [Caribe Travel Tours 2026](https://www.caribetraveltours.com/requisitos-de-viaje/panama-desde-colombia); [Citas y Trámites 2026](https://citasytramites.net/colombia/requisitos-para-viajar-a-panama-desde-colombia/).
- Paso Canoas in practice: one cycling guide says the onward-ticket rule is "not always enforced" and that the author was not asked when crossing from Costa Rica to Panama. Other travellers were turned away with "no onward travel no entry to Panama." One guide says showing a San José to Paso Canoas bus ticket is a "classic rookie mistake" because it only proves arrival at the border — [BikeHikeSafari, Bicycle Touring Panama](https://bikehikesafari.com/bicycle-touring-panama/) [undated]; [Functional Hippies, Paso Canoas guide](https://functionalhippies.com/paso-canoas-border-run/) [25 Jun 2025]; [Ticket To Utopia, Paso Canoas](https://tickettoutopia.com/paso-canoas/) [undated]. (These anecdotes come from one combined search summary of these guides, so the quote-to-source match is approximate.)
- Panama announced free medical insurance for tourists, but only for those who enter legally by air. It does not cover land arrivals [Sept 2026] — [Semana](https://www.semana.com/turismo/articulo/panama-ofrece-seguro-medico-gratuito-a-turistas-colombianos-desde-el-momento-de-su-llegada-asi-funcionara/202609/).
- Yellow fever: sources conflict. One guide lists vaccination at least 10 days before travel as a requirement, while another calls it "not mandatory" [2026 guides] — [Holafly](https://esim.holafly.com/es/blog/planificar-viaje/requisitos-viajar-colombia-panama/); [IATI](https://www.iatiglobal.com/co/blog/requisitos-viajar-panama-desde-colombia/).
- The most authoritative sources are Panama's Servicio Nacional de Migración (migracion.gob.pa) and the Panamanian Embassy in Colombia. The guides above are commercial — [Holafly](https://esim.holafly.com/es/blog/planificar-viaje/requisitos-viajar-colombia-panama/).

### Inferences
- For a couple entering Panama, each person should carry proof of at least USD 500 (bank statement or card) and a refundable or temporary onward booking that leaves Panama. Their real exit by bicycle into Costa Rica cannot be shown with a ticket. A bus ticket from Panama into Costa Rica (for example Tica Bus) or a refundable flight out of Costa Rica would satisfy the rule better than a border bus ticket.
- Bicycles are personal effects. The motorcycle insurance and temporary-import steps described in Paso Canoas motorcycle guides do not apply to bicycles, but I found no source that states this explicitly.

### Gaps
- I could not reach migracion.gob.pa to confirm officially the 90-day stay for Colombians, the USD 500 rule or the onward-ticket wording for land entries.
- I found no source for any Panamanian exit fee at Paso Canoas.
- How Panama treats cyclists arriving from Colombia by sea (Darién region / San Blas) belongs to another research thread and is not covered here.

## Costa Rica: visa category, exemptions through other visas, consular visa process from Colombia, onward ticket and funds, Paso Canoas and Peñas Blancas, land exit tax

### Takeaway
As of October 2026 Colombians still need a consular visa for Costa Rica. This is under the DGME Directrices Generales de Visas, Circular AJ-484-11-2025, published 17 Nov 2025. They are exempt if they hold a valid multiple-entry US visa (B1/B2, D, C1/D) or a multiple-entry Canadian visa or residence, valid at least 1 day on arrival. A multiple-entry Schengen C or D visa also exempts, as do certain EU, UK, Iceland, Norway and Switzerland residence permits. Colombia's new president asked Costa Rica on 22 Sep 2026 to drop the visa. President Laura Fernández agreed to study it, but no change has been announced.

### Cited Findings
**Category and legal basis**
- DGME published new general visa guidelines in Alcance N° 148 to La Gaceta N° 216 on 17 Nov 2025, Circular AJ-484-11-2025. In it the "third group" is nationalities requiring a consular visa — [DGME, Directrices Generales de Visas Nov 2025 (PDF)](https://migracion.go.cr/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Directrices-Generales-de-Visas-Noviembre-2025-Alcance-148-Gaceta-N%C2%B0-216-17-11-2025.pdf) [17 Nov 2025]; [Tico Times](https://ticotimes.net/2025/11/24/costa-rica-updates-visa-requirements-for-foreign-visitors) [24 Nov 2025].
- Colombia, Ecuador, Nicaragua, the Dominican Republic and Venezuela are in the consular-visa group — [El Financiero CR, "Costa Rica actualiza requisitos de ingreso"](https://www.elfinancierocr.com/economia-y-politica/costa-rica-actualiza-requisitos-de-ingreso-estos/N2IIWWNEDFBJ7NUUMBOKET4KIA/story/) [Nov 2025].
- The Costa Rican Embassy/Consulate in Bogotá states that, under Circular AJ-484-11-2025, "los ciudadanos colombianos y venezolanos requieren de visa para ingresar a Costa Rica" — [Embajada de Costa Rica en Colombia, Visas](https://www.embajadadecostarica.org/visas/) [2026].
- Older third-party guides that say Colombians need no visa for up to 90 or 180 days are wrong and outdated — contradicted by the Embassy and DGME above. Example of the outdated claim: [Era Viajera](https://www.eraviajera.com/visado-costa-rica-colombia/) and [visitcostarica.es](https://visitcostarica.es/blog/que-paises-no-necesitan-visa-para-entrar-a-costa-rica/).
- The visa requirement for Colombians dates from 15 April 2002 and was imposed for national-security and migration-control reasons (per search summary of the Sept 2026 coverage) — [Cronista](https://www.cronista.com/colombia/actualidad-co/colombia-solicito-a-costa-rica-eliminar-la-visa-para-ingresar-al-pais-el-gobierno-tico-analizara-la-propuesta/) [Sept 2026].

**Recent change and political context (2026)**
- On 22 Sep 2026 in Barranquilla, Colombian President Abelardo de la Espriella asked Costa Rican President Laura Fernández to remove the visa requirement, citing reciprocity (Colombia does not require visas from Costa Ricans) — [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/costa-rica/2026/09/22/abelardo-de-la-espriella-solicita-a-laura-fernandez-eliminar-el-requisito-de-visa-para-que-los-colombianos-ingresen-a-costa-rica/) [22 Sep 2026]; [La Nación](https://www.nacion.com/politica/presidente-de-colombia-pide-a-laura-fernandez/ZLU5MJWJEZGTJKDO3IN7BLAHNQ/story/) [Sept 2026].
- Fernández committed to study the change. Any decision must follow Costa Rica's internal procedures, and no date or formal process was announced — [CRC 891](https://crc891.com/nacionales/politica/laura-fernandez-se-compromete-con-de-la-espriella-a-estudiar-cambios-en-visa-para-colombianos) [Sept 2026]; [Semanario Universidad](https://semanariouniversidad.com/pais/colombia-pide-a-costa-rica-eliminar-visa-para-colombianos-de-la-espriella-dice-que-fernandez-revisara-propuesta/) [Sept 2026]; [Valora Analitik](https://www.valoraanalitik.com/colombia-pide-a-costa-rica-eliminar-visa-para-colombianos-y-avanzar-hacia-reciprocidad-migratoria/) [Sept 2026].
- A search for an October 2026 response found no official announcement eliminating the visa. The requirement remains in force — [Monumental](https://www.monumental.co.cr/2026/09/22/presidente-de-colombia-pidio-a-laura-fernandez-eliminar-requisito-de-visa-para-ciudadanos-colombianos/) [22 Sep 2026].

**Exemptions (no Costa Rican visa needed)**
- US or Canada: holders of a valid multiple-entry visa or residence from the US or Canada can skip the consular visa. The visa needs a minimum validity of 1 calendar day counted from the planned date of entry into Costa Rica — [DGME, Visas page](https://migracion.go.cr/visas/) [current]; [Embajada de Costa Rica, "Excepciones de visa consular" (PDF)](https://www.embajadadecostarica.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Excepciones-de-visa-Ingreso.pdf) [Mar 2026].
- Qualifying US visas: B1-B2 or B1/B2, type D, and C1/D, multiple entry. For Canada only the multiple-entry visa counts. US C1, C2 and C3 transit visas are not accepted — [Embajada de Costa Rica, Excepciones (PDF)](https://www.embajadadecostarica.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Excepciones-de-visa-Ingreso.pdf) [Mar 2026]; [Colombian consulate in San José, guía del inmigrante](https://sanjosedecostarica.consulado.gov.co/sites/default/files/news/attachments/guia-costa-rica.pdf) [undated].
- Schengen: a Schengen visa of category "C" with multiple entries, or category "D" with multiple entries, exempts the holder from the consular visa — [Embajada de Costa Rica, Excepciones (PDF)](https://www.embajadadecostarica.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Excepciones-de-visa-Ingreso.pdf) [Mar 2026]; [DGME Visas page](https://migracion.go.cr/visas/).
- Residence permits in Scotland, Wales, England, Northern Ireland (that is, the UK), Iceland, Norway, Switzerland or the EU also exempt, if multiple entry or valid at least 90 days. Refugee or asylum status does not count — [Embajada de Costa Rica, Excepciones (PDF)](https://www.embajadadecostarica.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Excepciones-de-visa-Ingreso.pdf) [Mar 2026].
- Japan, South Korea, Australia: older official texts and a university FAQ list exemptions for third-group nationals holding visas from the US, Canada, the Schengen area/EU, Japan or South Korea valid for 3 months, with a maximum stay of 30 days. These are older rules: the 2012 directive and a 2022 addendum. I could not confirm whether Japan, South Korea or Australia visas still exempt under the Nov 2025 directive — [UNA AICE FAQ](https://www.aice.una.ac.cr/index.php/preguntas-frecuentes/tramites-migratorios) [undated]; [PGR, Directrices 2012](http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/DOCS/NORMAS/1/VIGENTE/Z/2010-2019/2010-2014/2012/11B03/EDDC5.HTML) [2012, outdated]; [DGME addendum 2022 (PDF)](https://migracion.go.cr/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ADENDA-A-LAS-DIRECTRICES-GENERALES-DE-VISAS-CIRCULAR-DG-0027-09-2022-DG-UV.pdf) [2022].
- The minimum validity for US and Canada visas changed. A 2022 addendum required 3 months, and the current official page says 1 day, so the 1-day rule is newer and more authoritative — [DGME addendum 2022](https://migracion.go.cr/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ADENDA-A-LAS-DIRECTRICES-GENERALES-DE-VISAS-CIRCULAR-DG-0027-09-2022-DG-UV.pdf); [DGME Visas page](https://migracion.go.cr/visas/).
- Also exempt: airside layovers of under 12 hours without passing through migration — [Embajada de Costa Rica, Visas](https://www.embajadadecostarica.org/visas/).

**Length of stay (sources conflict)**
- One 2026 legal summary puts third-group nationals (including Colombia) at a stay of up to 30 days, extendable to 90, with a passport valid 180 days — [AG Legal, Costa Rica Visa Requirements 2026](https://aglegal.com/visa-for-costa-rica/) [2026]; the 2012 directive also gave 30 days extendable to 90 — [PGR 2012](http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/DOCS/NORMAS/1/VIGENTE/Z/2010-2019/2010-2014/2012/11B03/EDDC5.HTML).
- Against this, a news report on the Nov 2025 directive says that for Colombian and Nicaraguan citizens the required passport validity is reduced to 90 days and the legal stay can be up to 90 days. The Embassy also describes the single-entry tourism/business visa as being for stays under 90 days — [El Financiero CR / GDA](https://gda.com/detalle-de-la-noticia/?article=5931909) [Nov 2025]; [Embajada de Costa Rica, Visas](https://www.embajadadecostarica.org/visas/).
- Third and fourth group nationals must have a passport valid at least 6 months (180 calendar days) from the planned entry, per a summary of the Nov 2025 directive. This conflicts with the Colombia/Nicaragua 90-day carve-out reported by El Financiero and with the Embassy's "3 months" — [DGME Directrices Nov 2025 (PDF)](https://migracion.go.cr/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Directrices-Generales-de-Visas-Noviembre-2025-Alcance-148-Gaceta-N%C2%B0-216-17-11-2025.pdf). Authority ranking: the DGME directive PDF is the governing text. The Embassy and El Financiero describe it secondhand.

**Applying for the consular visa from Colombia (Embassy/Consulate in Bogotá)**
- The Embassy's tourist-visa requirements PDF (updated Feb 2026) lists:
  - an original passport valid at least 3 months;
  - an international yellow-fever certificate, vaccinated at least 10 days before;
  - payment of USD 30 in Colombian pesos to Banco de Occidente checking account Nº 228-02092-1;
  - a Colombian criminal-record certificate for migration purposes, apostilled through Cancillería (processed only through the "ENLACE" website);
  - a flight reservation in the applicant's name. Applicants are told not to buy the tickets.

  — [Embajada de Costa Rica, "Requisitos visa de turismo para único ingreso" (PDF)](https://www.embajadadecostarica.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Requisitos-visa-de-turismo-pagina-web-V02II-VI.pdf) [Feb 2026]. An earlier Dec 2024 version exists — [PDF Dec 2024](https://www.embajadadecostarica.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Requisitos-visa-de-turismo-pagina-web-DC2024.pdf).
- Visa validity: per the Embassy, single-entry tourist visas issued by the Bogotá consulate are valid 60 business days (Costa Rican calendar) from issuance. Per a summary of the Nov 2025 directive, the third-group single-entry visa must be stamped in the passport within 3 months once authorised. The two may describe different stages, authorisation versus use — [Embajada de Costa Rica, Visas](https://www.embajadadecostarica.org/visas/); [DGME Directrices Nov 2025](https://migracion.go.cr/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Directrices-Generales-de-Visas-Noviembre-2025-Alcance-148-Gaceta-N%C2%B0-216-17-11-2025.pdf).
- Proof of funds: the Colombian consulate in San José cites USD 100 per month of tourism — [Consulado de Colombia en San José, guía del inmigrante (PDF)](https://sanjosedecostarica.consulado.gov.co/sites/default/files/news/attachments/guia-costa-rica.pdf) [undated].
- Longer stays (study, work, residence) need a national visa processed at the Embassy or through authorised external centres — [Holafly 2026](https://esim.holafly.com/es/blog/planificar-viaje/requisitos-viajar-costa-rica-desde-colombia/).

**Onward ticket, land borders and exit tax**
- Cyclists have sometimes had to book an airline ticket to satisfy Costa Rica's onward-travel check and cancel it after crossing — [Pedals and Puffins, Cycling Across Costa Rica](https://www.pedalsandpuffins.com/americas-2022/2024/3/3/cycling-across-costa-rica-steamy-tropical-forests) [Mar 2024].
- Costa Rica's land exit tax (paid when leaving toward Nicaragua or Panama): USD 8 to 11 depending on payment method. It is about USD 8 online and USD 10 at the booths, and paying online before arriving is cheapest [2026 guide] — [Costa Rica Prática, Frontera Costa Rica-Nicaragua 2026](https://costaricapratica.com/es/frontera-costa-rica-nicaragua-guia-practica/); a Paso Canoas motorcycle guide also cites a Costa Rica exit tax of USD 8 to 10 — [TerraXplor, Paso Canoas by motorcycle](https://www.terraxplor.com/post/costa-rica-panama-paso-canoas) [undated].
- A 2026 commercial page about the Costa Rica–Panama border (Paso Canoas and Sixaola) sells temporary onward tickets (~USD 14). Treat its claims cautiously — [Volward](https://volward.com/en/blog/costa-rica-panama-border-crossing-paso-canoas-sixaola-2026) [2026].

### Inferences
- Case A, the couple has a valid multiple-entry US visa (B1/B2) or Canadian visa: they can enter Costa Rica at Paso Canoas without a Costa Rican visa, provided the visa is valid at least 1 day past entry. US visas are typically valid 10 years, so this is easy.
- Case B, no qualifying visa: they must get the Costa Rican consular visa in Bogotá before leaving Colombia. It is valid only about 60 business days from issuance or must be used within 3 months. A slow ride through Panama could therefore make it expire before they reach Paso Canoas. They should time the application against their expected arrival date at the Costa Rican border.
- The visa file asks for a flight reservation. Overland cyclists will need a reservation (not a purchase) that shows exit from Costa Rica.
- Because the stay length for Colombians is disputed (30 vs 90 days), the couple should plan the Costa Rica leg to fit inside 30 days unless the Embassy confirms 90 days in writing.

### Gaps
- I could not open the Nov 2025 DGME directive or the Embassy PDFs to confirm exact wording: stay length for Colombians, the full list of exemptions (Japan, South Korea, Australia, UK visas as opposed to UK residence), and the stay granted to exempt travellers.
- Consular visa processing time in Bogotá: no source found.
- Whether a bus ticket out of Costa Rica is accepted at Paso Canoas as proof of onward travel: only anecdotal reports.
- Peñas Blancas (exit from Costa Rica): only the exit tax was found. No cyclist-specific reports for 2026.

## Nicaragua: visa category, process, fees, advance notification, political risks, land borders, refusals

### Takeaway
Since February 2026 Colombians need a "visa consultada" (Category C) for Nicaragua. Disposición 002-2026 moved Colombia from Category B to Category C. The visa must be approved before travel and is applied for online on the Ministerio del Interior (MINT) portal. It is valid about 30 days from notification. Raizales from San Andrés are exempt. On top of the visa, travellers report a USD 10 to 14 tourist-card fee and a USD 1 municipal fee at entry, plus USD 4 to 5 at exit. One 2026 report says an online migration form must be sent 7 days before travel. Drones are banned by law, and cameras and devices may be searched or confiscated. Content creators have been refused entry.

### Cited Findings
**Visa category and dates**
- Disposición 002-2026 moved Colombia from migratory Category B to Category C. From 13 Feb 2026 Colombians need a visa consultada, applied for through Nicaragua's Embassy in Colombia and approved before the travel date. Colombian raizales from the San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina archipelago are exempt — [Consulado de Colombia en Managua, "Nuevo requisito de Visa para colombianos"](https://managua.consulado.gov.co/sala-de-prensa/noticias/nuevo-requisito-de-visa-para-colombianos-que-ingresen-nicaragua) [Feb 2026].
- Sources differ on the date in force. Some say Disposición 002-2026 took effect on 16 Feb 2026 (when it applied to Mexico and Peru), while Colombia's change applied from 13 Feb 2026. In total, 128 nationalities need a Category C visa — [La Prensa (Nicaragua)](https://www.laprensani.com/2026/06/30/nacionales/3725491-requisitos-ingresar-nicaragua-extranjeros) [30 Jun 2026]; [Vanguardia](https://www.vanguardia.com/mundo/2026/02/13/nicaragua-vuelve-a-exigir-visa-a-128-paises-colombia-esta-en-la-lista/) [13 Feb 2026]; [Artículo 66](https://www.articulo66.com/2026/02/13/estos-son-los-paises-que-requeriran-visa-para-ingresar-a-nicaragua/) [13 Feb 2026]; [El Diario de Hoy](https://www.elsalvador.com/noticias/internacional/nicaragua-visas/1262519/2026/) [2026]; [Travel And Tour World](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/k4eva6fs9gh8/) [2026].
- Older guides describing a roughly USD 10 airport or arrival visa or tourist card for Colombians, with no prior visa, are outdated — [Holafly (outdated in this respect)](https://esim.holafly.com/es/blog/planificar-viaje/requisitos-viajar-colombia-nicaragua/) [2026 but stale].

**Process**
- From 23 Feb 2026 the Category C visa process is entirely online, including fee payment. The visa is issued electronically and needs no physical stamp in the passport — [Dentons](https://www.dentons.com/en/insights/articles/2026/march/10/nicaragua-updates-its-immigration-regime-and-digitizes-the-consulted-visa-process) [10 Mar 2026].
- Portal: tramitesconsulares.mint.gob.ni. Applicants register with a valid email, fill in personal data and upload a face photo. After applying, the cost is emailed with a secure payment link (Visa or Mastercard). Approved visas can be verified at evisa-verifica.mint.gob.ni with the reference code — [La Prensa (Nicaragua)](https://www.laprensani.com/2026/06/30/nacionales/3725491-requisitos-ingresar-nicaragua-extranjeros) [30 Jun 2026]; [MINT informative PDF](https://www.mint.gob.ni/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/InformativoVISA.V1.pdf) [Feb 2026]; [MINT, Visa de ingreso al país](https://www.mint.gob.ni/migracion/tramites/visa-de-ingreso-al-pais/).
- Documents: a copy of a passport valid at least 6 months and a visa request letter. After approval, one source describes a guarantee deposit equal to the value of a return ticket and a notarised maintenance commitment. That source may refer to Cuban applicants specifically, so its application to Colombians is unconfirmed — [La Prensa (Nicaragua)](https://www.laprensani.com/2026/06/30/nacionales/3725491-requisitos-ingresar-nicaragua-extranjeros) [30 Jun 2026]; [Divergentes, "La visa consultada: así funciona"](https://www.divergentes.com/la-visa-consultada-asi-funciona-el-requisito-que-tramita-el-ministerio-del-interior/) [undated].
- Validity: the visa is valid 30 days from notification — [Consortium Legal](https://consortiumlegal.com/2026/02/19/actualizacion-de-categorias-migratorias-de-visa-y-nuevo-procedimiento-para-visas-consultadas-en-nicaragua/) [19 Feb 2026]; [Aviatur](https://www.aviatur.com/documentacion-visas/NI) [undated].
- Fee: Aviatur says USD 30 including the form, paid in exact cash at the Embassy on the appointment day. This likely predates the Feb 2026 online system, so it may be outdated. Another source cites "USD 30 + fee." The current amount is shown only during the online application — [Aviatur](https://www.aviatur.com/documentacion-visas/NI) [undated]; [La Prensa (Nicaragua)](https://www.laprensani.com/2026/06/30/nacionales/3725491-requisitos-ingresar-nicaragua-extranjeros) [30 Jun 2026].
- The Colombian consulate in Managua refers applicants to the Nicaraguan Embassy in Bogotá for forms, criminal records, cost and procedure — [Consulado de Colombia en Managua](https://managua.consulado.gov.co/sala-de-prensa/noticias/nuevo-requisito-de-visa-para-colombianos-que-ingresen-nicaragua) [Feb 2026].

**Entry and exit fees, advance notification**
- Foreigners pay USD 10 to enter Nicaragua and receive a tourist permit. They must also fill in an online migration form and send it 7 days before travel, and enter through an authorised border post — [La Prensa (Nicaragua)](https://www.laprensani.com/2026/06/30/nacionales/3725491-requisitos-ingresar-nicaragua-extranjeros) [30 Jun 2026].
- Traveller guide figures at Peñas Blancas: USD 12 to 14 entry fee plus a USD 1 municipal fee paid separately before passport control. Some report a "surprise" extra charge, which one source puts at USD 50 for certain passports. Exit from Nicaragua costs about USD 4 to 5 in total (exit fee plus municipal dollar). Carry small US-dollar bills [2026 guide] — [Costa Rica Prática, Frontera Costa Rica–Nicaragua 2026](https://costaricapratica.com/es/frontera-costa-rica-nicaragua-guia-practica/). Older blogs cite a USD 10 tourist fee plus a USD 2 Gobernación charge, or USD 12 each way. These are outdated (more than 3 years old) — [Furgo en ruta](https://www.furgoenruta.com/frontera-de-penas-blancas-de-costa-rica-a-nicaragua/); [Nomadic Backpacker](https://www.nomadicbackpacker.com/border-crossing-costa-rica-nicaragua-penas-blancas.html).
- An Argentine motorcycle YouTuber riding to Alaska, Pablo Imhoff, was refused entry at Peñas Blancas because of an "incomplete form." He disputed this, saying Argentine friends entered without completing it (date not shown in the results) — [100% Noticias](https://100noticias.tv/nacionales/127953-niegan-ingreso-nicaragua-youtuber-argentino/).

**Political situation, drones, cameras, content creators**
- Drones: Ley No. 1230, approved 11 Dec 2024 and published two days later, bans the entry and use of unmanned aircraft by individuals and companies, with exceptions for official use. Without prior authorisation, travellers cannot obtain one on arrival — [Diario Las Américas](https://www.diariolasamericas.com/america-latina/daniel-ortega-prohibe-uso-drones-nicaragua-a-que-le-teme-n5368073) [Dec 2024]; [Asados la Brocha, Drones en Nicaragua](https://asadoslabrocha.com/blog/drones-en-nicaragua) [undated].
- A photographer had his drone confiscated at Managua airport in Dec 2024 and received a receipt to reclaim it on leaving — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2025/09/04/i-got-my-camera-drone-confiscated-in-nicaragua-lesson-learned/) [4 Sep 2025].
- Professional cameras can be confiscated on suspicion of documenting social conditions without permission. Bringing a drone can lead to criminal proceedings. Wearing only blue and white (the flag colours) can be read as political opposition — [Nicaragua Investiga, "reglas para viajar a Nicaragua cambian drásticamente"](https://nicaraguainvestiga.com/nacion/167273-lo-que-debe-saber-reglas-para-viajar-a-nicaragua-cambian-drasticamente/) [18 Dec 2025].
- Any audiovisual or film activity needs prior authorisation from the Cinemateca Nacional. Night-vision binoculars and drones are banned — [Confidencial](https://confidencial.digital/nacion/viajar-a-nicaragua-consejo-ante-negativa-de-ingreso-y-prohibiciones/) [2025].
- A bus operator said Nicaragua does not allow drones, cameras, Bibles, newspapers, magazines or books of any kind. The Bible ban affects tourists entering by land from Costa Rica — [CSW](https://www.csw.org.uk/2025/12/12/press/6695/article.htm) [12 Dec 2025]; [AICA](https://aica.org/noticia-nicaragua-prohibe-a-los-turistas-traer-biblias-al-pais) [Dec 2025].
- The US State Department advisory says officials may search phones, computers and social media for anti-government content and may confiscate devices. Travellers can be refused entry or prevented from leaving without explanation — [US State Department, Nicaragua Travel Advisory](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/nicaragua-travel-advisory.html) [current].
- Refusals of content creators: Peruvian YouTuber Henry Alexander Guillón said in Sept 2024 that he was stopped at a bus terminal in Costa Rica because his entry was denied. Peruvian Andy Tirado said he was held 6 hours at the border and questioned about his itinerary before being admitted. Nicaraguan YouTuber Chico Reyes Rosas was barred from returning — [La Prensa (Nicaragua), "Los youtubers que han expuesto la realidad de Nicaragua"](https://www.laprensani.com/2025/05/26/politica/3477753-los-youtubers-que-han-expuesto-la-realidad-de-nicaragua-desde-2018) [26 May 2025]; [Nicaragua Investiga](https://nicaraguainvestiga.com/nacion/153656-dictadura-niega-entrada-nicaragua-youtuber-nicaraguense-chico-reyes/) [2025].
- A "Grupo de Expertos" cited by Confidencial (probably the UN Group of Human Rights Experts on Nicaragua, not verified) reports 318 Nicaraguans were denied entry between June 2018 and August 2025. Refusals mainly target people seen as a "risk," not equipment — [Confidencial](https://confidencial.digital/nacion/viajar-a-nicaragua-consejo-ante-negativa-de-ingreso-y-prohibiciones/) [2025].
- The final decision on entry always rests with Nicaragua, and entry can be denied even to someone who previously travelled without problems — [Destino USA, Viajar a Nicaragua 2026](https://destinousa.com/viajar-nicaragua/) [2026].

### Inferences
- The Colombian couple must apply for the visa consultada online well before reaching Peñas Blancas. Because it is valid only about 30 days from notification, they should apply when they are 2 to 4 weeks from the Nicaraguan border, which can be done while in Costa Rica. They should also submit the online pre-travel migration form at least 7 days before the crossing date.
- A cycling couple with a public blog or social-media channel is a risk profile. They should not carry a drone, keep camera gear modest, and consider what is visible on their phones and social media.
- Crossing Nicaragua is short, about 300 to 350 km between Peñas Blancas and Guasaule or El Espino. Even so, the new visa, the advance form, fees, and inspection risk make Nicaragua the most bureaucratically demanding country on this route in 2026. The ferry that once allowed skipping it is not running (next section).

## The ferry between Costa Rica (Caldera) and El Salvador (La Unión)

### Takeaway
No ferry operates between Costa Rica and El Salvador in October 2026. The only service, Blue Wave Corporation's ship "Blue Wave Harmony" between Puerto La Unión and Puerto Caldera, ran mainly as a cargo ferry from August to November 2023. Its passenger service never launched. It has been suspended indefinitely since late 2023, and as of the latest reports no operator wants the route. Travellers cannot use it to skip Nicaragua and Honduras.

### Cited Findings
- Operator and ship: Blue Wave Corporation, with the vessel Blue Wave Harmony. Operations began 10 Aug 2023, and the first trip reached Caldera on Friday 11 Aug 2023 — [CRHoy](https://crhoy.com/nacionales/primer-viaje-de-ferry-entre-el-salvador-y-costa-rica-llegara-a-caldera-el-viernes/) [Aug 2023]; [INCOP](https://incop.go.cr/noticias/cr-es-inicio-operaciones-ferry/) [Aug 2023]; [Semanario Universidad](https://semanariouniversidad.com/pais/ferry-para-carga-entre-el-salvador-y-puerto-caldera-iniciara-operaciones-el-10-de-agosto/) [2023].
- Schedule and design: twice a week (later cut to once a week), 16 to 18 hours per crossing, capacity about 100 trailers. It was meant to reduce congestion at Peñas Blancas — [La República](https://www.larepublica.net/noticia/ferry-de-carga-entre-el-salvador-y-costa-rica-arranca-operaciones-en-agosto) [2023]; [Semanario Universidad](https://semanariouniversidad.com/pais/mopt-esta-en-conversaciones-con-empresas-panamena-y-mexicana-para-restaurar-ferri-entre-costa-rica-y-el-salvador/) [early 2024].
- Fares in 2023: USD 1,145 to 1,360 per freight unit (truck, trailer and driver) depending on length and weight. No per-passenger or per-bicycle fare was ever published — [Revista E&N](https://www.revistaeyn.com/centroamericaymundo/ferry-costa-rica-el-salvador-tendra-tarifas-de-entre-us-1145-y-us-1360-FG14654212) [Jul 2023]; [La Prensa Gráfica](https://www.laprensagrafica.com/economia/Fijan-la-fecha-y-las-tarifas-para-inicio-de-operaciones-del-ferri-20230730-0056.html) [30 Jul 2023]; [Diario El Mundo SV](https://diario.elmundo.sv/economia/estos-seran-los-horarios-y-costos-del-ferri-que-conectara-el-salvador-con-costa-rica) [2023].
- Passengers: the ship can carry 200 passengers in 73 cabins (2, 3 and 4 berths). Passenger service was planned for a "second phase," and its price was never announced — [Yahoo Noticias / EFE, "Blue Wave Harmony, el único transbordador de carga"](https://es-us.noticias.yahoo.com/blue-wave-harmony-%C3%BAnico-transbordador-220929357.html) [2023]; [Revista E&N](https://www.revistaeyn.com/centroamericaymundo/ferry-el-salvador-costa-rica-genera-grandes-expectativas-para-el-comercio-en-centroamerica-FN14802214) [2023].
- The route launched with only about 10% capacity used, never exceeded a quarter of capacity, and the Costa Rican government subsidised its start with USD 2 million — [Costa Rican Times](https://www.costaricantimes.com/groundbreaking-maritime-connection-between-costa-rica-and-el-salvador-launches-with-only-10-capacity/74842) [2023]; [Semanario Universidad](https://semanariouniversidad.com/pais/mopt-esta-en-conversaciones-con-empresas-panamena-y-mexicana-para-restaurar-ferri-entre-costa-rica-y-el-salvador/) [early 2024].
- Suspension: service stopped in November 2023. MOPT confirmed the suspension on 19 Dec 2023, the operator suspended indefinitely, and the ship left for the Atlantic — [CRHoy](https://crhoy.com/nacionales/ferry-el-salvador-costa-rica-esta-suspendido-confirma-mopt/) [Dec 2023]; [Delfino](https://delfino.cr/2023/12/ferry-a-el-salvador-dejo-de-operar-desde-noviembre-mopt-espera-comunicado-oficial) [Dec 2023]; [La Nación](https://www.nacion.com/economia/negocios/ferri-costa-rica-el-salvador-suspende-operaciones/LGS75NYRAJBAHDEMBP43JPWZNM/story/) [Dec 2023]; [Voz de América](https://www.vozdeamerica.com/a/suspende-operaciones-ferry-que-conectaba-el-salvador-costa-rica-escasa-demanda-de-trayectos/7407366.html) [Dec 2023].
- Attempts to restart: MOPT held preliminary talks with Panamanian and Mexican shipping companies in early 2024. In July 2024 an Ecuadorian company showed interest but asked for tax exemptions that could not be granted. As of 22 Aug 2025, MOPT said there were no private operators interested, after 21 months suspended. Both governments remain interested, and there were very preliminary talks with Guatemala — [La Nación, "Operación suma 21 meses suspendida"](https://www.nacion.com/economia/que-se-sabe-del-ferri-entre-costa-rica-y-el/MNTVTFAH7RHGXCQV4G3S4ODTDI/story/) [Aug 2025]; [CRHoy, "Ferri a El Salvador suma 9 meses en parálisis"](https://crhoy.com/nacionales/ferri-a-el-salvador-suma-9-meses-en-paralisis-no-tenemos-e/) [~Aug 2024].
- A Digital506 item dated 30 Apr 2026 says the ferry "arrancará operaciones en agosto." Its text matches the July 2023 announcement and its metadata suggests it is about 3 years old, so it is almost certainly misdated. No other source confirms a 2026 restart — [Digital506](https://digital506.com/ferry-de-carga-entre-el-salvador-costa-rica-arranca-operaciones-en-agosto/) [dated 30 Apr 2026, suspect].

### Inferences
- For the couple: plan to ride through Nicaragua (and Honduras), or fly with boxed bikes from San José or Liberia to San Salvador to skip both. There is no sea option in 2026.
- If the ferry ever restarts, it would most likely be freight-only at first, given that the passenger phase never launched.

### Gaps
- No 2026 MOPT, INCOP or CEPA statement was found, so "still suspended in October 2026" rests on the August 2025 La Nación report plus no evidence of a restart.
- I found no evidence that the 2023 service ever took foot passengers or bicycles.

## CA-4 agreement (Guatemala, El Salvador, Honduras, Nicaragua): how the shared 90 days works for Colombians

### Takeaway
Guatemala, El Salvador and Honduras admit Colombians visa-free for tourism. Under CA-4 the four countries act as one territory: the 90-day clock starts in the first CA-4 country entered and does not reset at internal borders. Extensions are possible, mainly through Guatemala's IGM. Nicaragua is in CA-4 but now requires its own visa consultada for Colombians.

### Cited Findings
- Colombian tourists need no visa for Guatemala and can stay up to 90 days within the CA-4. Proof of exit from the country, such as a return ticket, is required [2026 guide] — [Holafly, Guatemala desde Colombia](https://esim.holafly.com/es/blog/planificar-viaje/requisitos-viajar-colombia-guatemala/).
- A 2026 Colombian outlet lists Guatemala, El Salvador and Honduras among Central American countries that do not require a visa from Colombians for short stays, "generalmente hasta 90 días." Nicaragua is not on the list — [La FM](https://www.lafm.com.co/sociedad/colombianos-viajar-sin-visa-a-centroamerica-solo-con-pasaporte-2026-390998) [2026].
- The four countries act as one territory for visitors. Visa-free travellers get 90 days for the whole region, not 90 per country. The clock starts at the first CA-4 country entered and does not reset when crossing internal borders — [Maprecap CA-4 calculator](https://maprecap.com/ca4-calculator/) [undated]; [Pack a Sandwich](https://www.packasandwich.com/en/discussions/how-does-the-ca-4-agreement-work-for-traveling-in-central-america-1071/) [undated]; [Rouletout, CA4 rules](https://www.rouletout.ch/ca4-countries) [undated].
- Extension: sources differ. The UK Foreign Office mentions up to 30 days, while travellers report up to 90 more days granted by Guatemala's Instituto Guatemalteco de Migración — [Pack a Sandwich / Maprecap search summary](https://www.packasandwich.com/en/discussions/how-does-the-ca-4-agreement-work-for-traveling-in-central-america-1071/) [undated].
- Nicaragua now requires Colombians to hold a visa consultada (see the Nicaragua section) — [Consulado de Colombia en Managua](https://managua.consulado.gov.co/sala-de-prensa/noticias/nuevo-requisito-de-visa-para-colombianos-que-ingresen-nicaragua) [Feb 2026].

### Inferences
- Northbound from Costa Rica, the CA-4 clock would start at Peñas Blancas (Nicaragua), assuming Nicaragua counts the days as CA-4 time. The couple would then have about 90 days in total for Nicaragua, Honduras, El Salvador and Guatemala, so a slow cycling pace needs planning or an extension in Guatemala.
- I found no source on whether Nicaragua's 30-day visa consultada limits the time spent in Nicaragua itself (inside the CA-4 total) or only the window for entering.

### Gaps
- No official CA-4 source (Guatemala's IGM, El Salvador's DGME, Honduras's INM) was retrieved. All CA-4 details come from blogs and aggregators.
- How Nicaragua's new Category C visa interacts with the CA-4 shared stay was not found in any source.
